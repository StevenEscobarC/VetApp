<objective>
Close the quote loop and the void-then-reissue loop:
- edit an open quote;
- convert a quote into a cuenta de cobro in one tap (with the stock deducted at that moment);
- reissue a voided CC as a new one.
The detail's action table becomes complete for every state.

## Phase Goal

**As a** veterinario independiente o de una clínica pequeña, **I want to** cobrar mis consultas, servicios y productos con una cotización o una cuenta de cobro en PDF que comparto por WhatsApp, **so that** el cliente sepa cuánto debe, yo sepa qué está pagado y el inventario se descuente solo.

Recorded choice: "Emitir una nueva" copies the visit link (citaId/consultaId) along with client, pet and lines. Voiding released the visit (07-01 T21), so the reissued CC re-links it and the "Cobrada" rows keep working. There is no link to the voided document itself (UI-SPEC §4).
Output: draft copy method, three form modes, two routes, the full detail action table and tests.
</objective>
