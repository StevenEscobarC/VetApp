<objective>
Billing an inventory product deducts stock (BILL-03) from the phone. The slice adds the active-product picker, stock-aware lines with the D-12 blocking message, server stock errors mapped onto the exact line, and the inventory history labels for sales and voids. The deduction itself is atomic on the server (07-01); this slice makes it visible and safe in the UI.

## Phase Goal

**As a** veterinario independiente o de una clínica pequeña, **I want to** cobrar mis consultas, servicios y productos con una cotización o una cuenta de cobro en PDF que comparto por WhatsApp, **so that** el cliente sepa cuánto debe, yo sepa qué está pagado y el inventario se descuente solo.

Output: product picker sheet, draft/product methods, stock UI on lines, movement labels and tests. CobrosActions already invalidate inventory (07-02).
</objective>
