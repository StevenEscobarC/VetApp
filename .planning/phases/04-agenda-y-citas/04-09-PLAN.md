<objective>
Sixth slice: completing a cita leads into the clinical record — per-mascota consulta registration prefilled from the cita and formally linked to it, optional skipping, "Completar sin consulta", and estado completada with Deshacer (AGND-06 and the "completada" part of AGND-03; D-17, D-18, D-19).

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente que atiende sin recepcionista, **I want to** ver mi agenda por día/semana, agendar citas para una o varias mascotas de un cliente, cambiar su estado, recibir un recordatorio en el celular, recordarle la cita al cliente por WhatsApp con un toque y registrar la consulta al completar la cita, **so that** gestione mi agenda y avise a mis clientes sin depender de una recepcionista ni de un computador.

Purpose: After this plan a vet finishes a visit from the agenda and the consulta lands in the patient's history linked to the cita. Completion is NOT one transaction with the consultas (RESEARCH Pattern 2): each consulta is durable and append-only; Finalizar only updates estado. Runs in parallel with 04-10 (no shared files).
Output: consulta repository/provider/form changes + tests, CompletarCitaScreen + routes, Completar entry points, completed-detail rows, tests.
</objective>
