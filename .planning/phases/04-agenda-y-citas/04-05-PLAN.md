<objective>
Second vertical slice: the vet creates a cita end to end — "Nueva cita" on the Agenda (and "Agendar cita" from fichas, wired in 04-04) opens the form; cliente search, multi-mascota selection, motivo chips with default durations, first-free-slot time stepper, domicilio with prefilled dirección, notes, overlap warning, combined alta return; saving calls `crear_cita` and lands back on the Agenda day of the cita (AGND-02; D-01..D-07, D-09, D-21).

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente que atiende sin recepcionista, **I want to** ver mi agenda por día/semana, agendar citas para una o varias mascotas de un cliente, cambiar su estado, recibir un recordatorio en el celular, recordarle la cita al cliente por WhatsApp con un toque y registrar la consulta al completar la cita, **so that** gestione mi agenda y avise a mis clientes sin depender de una recepcionista ni de un computador.

Purpose: After this plan a real user can book appointments. It also introduces `CitaActions` and `citasRevisionProvider` (the write path and the "citas changed" signal the reminder slice listens to).
Output: overlap/free-slot utilities + tests, repository `crear`, actions/providers, CitaFormScreen + widgets, routes, Agenda "Nueva cita" button and overlap label, tests.
</objective>
