<objective>
First vertical slice of the phase: the vet opens the Agenda tab and sees their real week — day strip LUN–DOM with per-day counts, today's citas by hour, week navigation, "Próxima" banner and proper loading/empty/error states (AGND-01, D-08). Includes the Bogotá time helpers and Spanish time formatters every later slice uses, the reconciled `Cita` entity, the read side of `SupabaseCitaRepository`, and the providers.

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente que atiende sin recepcionista, **I want to** ver mi agenda por día/semana, agendar citas para una o varias mascotas de un cliente, cambiar su estado, recibir un recordatorio en el celular, recordarle la cita al cliente por WhatsApp con un toque y registrar la consulta al completar la cita, **so that** gestione mi agenda y avise a mis clientes sin depender de una recepcionista ni de un computador.

Purpose: Runs in parallel with 04-01 (schema) against `FakeCitaRepository`; on device it reads live citas once 04-01's checkpoint passes. The entity, repository select, provider keys and helper names defined here are the contract for 04-05..04-10.
