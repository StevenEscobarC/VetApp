<objective>
Third slice: the vet manages cita estados and details — Confirmar / No asistió / Cancelar / Reabrir with Deshacer, a full cita detail screen, and editing (AGND-03; D-10, D-11, D-09 on edit). Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente que atiende sin recepcionista, **I want to** ver mi agenda por día/semana, agendar citas para una o varias mascotas de un cliente, cambiar su estado, recibir un recordatorio en el celular, recordarle la cita al cliente por WhatsApp con un toque y registrar la consulta al completar la cita, **so that** gestione mi agenda y avise a mis clientes sin depender de una recepcionista ni de un computador.

Purpose: After this plan a real user can confirm, mark no-show, cancel, reopen and edit citas. "Completar" (estado completada) arrives with its consulta flow in 04-09; WhatsApp/Cómo llegar buttons in 04-08.
Output: repository + actions write methods, shared action helpers, CitaDetailScreen, card actions, edit mode, routes, tests.

| T-04-23 | Tampering | actualizar_cita mascota swap / terminal edit | mitigate | RPC guards from 04-01 (same owner, estado pendiente/confirmada, cannot drop a mascota with linked consulta); client also hides edit for terminal estados |
| T-04-24 | Repudiation / data loss | Cancel | mitigate | Cancel is an estado change with confirmation dialog and Deshacer; no delete method exists in the repository or RLS |
