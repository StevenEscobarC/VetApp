<objective>
Make the client side of scheduling work: Colombian phone normalization at save time with a soft warning (D-16), "Agendar cita" entry points on the mascota and cliente fichas (D-07), and a return-result mode on the existing combined alta so the new-cita form can create a cliente + mascota and come back with them selected (D-04).

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente que atiende sin recepcionista, **I want to** ver mi agenda por día/semana, agendar citas para una o varias mascotas de un cliente, cambiar su estado, recibir un recordatorio en el celular, recordarle la cita al cliente por WhatsApp con un toque y registrar la consulta al completar la cita, **so that** gestione mi agenda y avise a mis clientes sin depender de una recepcionista ni de un computador.

Purpose: Independent of the agenda files, so it runs in Wave 1. `telefono_co.dart` is consumed by the WhatsApp slice (04-08); the return-mode and query-param contracts are consumed by the new-cita form (04-05). User-visible now: phones are saved in one consistent format with a helpful warning, and the fichas have "Agendar cita".
Output: telefono_co.dart + tests; updated combined alta, cliente detail, mascota detail + their tests.
</objective>
