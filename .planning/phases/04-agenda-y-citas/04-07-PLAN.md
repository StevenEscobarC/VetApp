<objective>
Fourth slice: the vet's phone reminds them before each cita (AGND-04; D-12 default lead, Claude's Discretion on package, inexact mode, idempotent reschedule, cancelAll on sign-out, Bogotá timezone). Pure planning (which citas, when, ids, texts) is separated from the plugin call so it is unit-tested; the service sits behind an interface so tests and desktop targets never touch the plugin.

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente que atiende sin recepcionista, **I want to** ver mi agenda por día/semana, agendar citas para una o varias mascotas de un cliente, cambiar su estado, recibir un recordatorio en el celular, recordarle la cita al cliente por WhatsApp con un toque y registrar la consulta al completar la cita, **so that** gestione mi agenda y avise a mis clientes sin depender de una recepcionista ni de un computador.

Purpose: After this plan, on an Android phone with notifications already allowed, reminders fire for every upcoming cita and follow every change.
