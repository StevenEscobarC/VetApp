**Whole Phase 5.1 server contract appended to `supabase/schema.sql` as one idempotent block: plan table, single `_plan_efectivo` derivation, Beta backfill, Pro-trial trigger, LP001..LP004 enforcement triggers, seat-aware invitations, logo-hiding carne and the service_role-only `asignar_plan`. Static grep gates pass; nothing was executed against a live database.**

## Accomplishments

- **Plan model (Task 1):** `clinica_planes` with the three named constraints, select-only RLS and revokes; Beta backfill as a single guarded statement (one occurrence in the file, line before the trigger); `_plan_efectivo(p_clinica, p_hoy)` producing the 13-column row with rank order Beta > Clinica > Pro > Gratis, paid-over-trial tie-break, gracia only for paid plans and the Clinica trial, `ultimo_vencimiento/ultimo_origen` only when falling back to Gratis; `clinicas_crear_plan` AFTER INSERT trigger (30-day Pro trial); `puede_escribir`, `plan_permite_logo`, `mi_plan` (exactly 21 keys, `miembros_max` null for Beta) and `asignar_plan` (42501 when `auth.uid()` is not null, tramos 2/3/5, notice when members exceed seats, grant to service_role only).
- **Enforcement (Task 2):** `mascotas_validar_limite` (advisory lock, LP001), `clinicas_validar_logo` with WHEN clause (LP003), `protocolos_validar_plan` (activo-only updates pass, seed rows pass, LP003), `guard_solo_lectura` on clientes, mascotas, mascota_pesos, citas, cita_mascotas, consultas, dosis_aplicadas (LP004, reminder marking exempt, returns OLD on DELETE); five storage policies redefined (`clinica_logos_insert/update` + `plan_permite_logo()`, `mascota_fotos_insert/update/delete` + `puede_escribir()`).
- **Redefinitions (Task 3):** `generar_invitacion_clinica` (locks the plan row, opens the one-time Clinica trial or raises LP002), `consumir_invitacion` (three P0001 texts byte-identical, LP002 seat check, no grants), `carne_de_mascota` and `carne_publico` generated by copying the Fase 5 bodies and replacing only the `logo_path` expression (same five clinica keys, grants re-affirmed); closing grants block.
- Nine `-- redefinida en Fase 5.1 (plan)` pointer comments added next to the originals (5 storage policies + 4 functions); nothing else outside the block changed.
