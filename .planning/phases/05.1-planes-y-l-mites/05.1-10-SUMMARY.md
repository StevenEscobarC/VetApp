# Phase 5.1 Plan 10: Read-only colleagues on agenda and cita form Summary

**Read-only colleagues keep the agenda, reminders, directions and history, but every cita write shows a lock and opens the 'Tu cuenta está en solo lectura' sheet; the cita form never creates a patient past the limit and a stale LP00x on save ends in the matching sheet with the form intact. `flutter analyze` clean, `flutter test` 849/849 passing.**

- Cita card: 'Completar', 'Confirmar' and the Más acciones entries locked; WhatsApp and Cómo llegar unchanged.
- Cita detail: 'Editar cita', 'Reasignar cita', 'Completar cita', 'Confirmar', 'No asistió', 'Cancelar cita', 'Reabrir cita', 'Registrar consulta pendiente' locked; 'Ver historia clínica', WhatsApp and Cómo llegar unchanged.
