<objective>
Third vertical slice: the vet exports the whole clinical history of a patient to PDF from the ficha and shares/saves it through the phone's native share sheet (HIST-03, D-04 whole history, D-05 vet-only native share).

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente, **I want to** registrar consultas estructuradas, ver la línea de tiempo y exportar la historia clínica de cada paciente desde el celular, **so that** lleve una historia clínica digital trazable y exportable sin papel ni computador.

Purpose: Makes the history "exportable" (phase goal) with zero extra ceremony. Fonts and the share call are injected through providers (same pattern as `capturadorFotoProvider`) so tests never hit the network or a platform channel.
Output: PDF service, providers, top-bar export action, unit + widget tests.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-PDF | Information Disclosure | exported PDF shared to the wrong recipient | accept | D-05: vet-only, native share by explicit tap; no public link, no upload, no owner-facing channel; social risk outside code control (RESEARCH Security Domain) |
| T-03-PDFDATA | Information Disclosure | PDF content scope | mitigate | Built only from RLS-filtered consultasProvider/mascotaProvider data of the vet's own clinic; no veterinarian identity or other patients included |
| T-03-FONT | Tampering / Denial of Service | network font fetch | mitigate | Fonts come from the printing package's PdfGoogleFonts (HTTPS, same trust model as google_fonts already in the app); any failure becomes a Spanish error, never a crash |
| T-03-ERR | Information Disclosure | error text | mitigate | Service and share wrapper convert every exception to a fixed ConsultaFailure message |
