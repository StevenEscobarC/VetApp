<objective>
Seventh vertical slice: the returning-owner path and pet editing. An existing client brings another pet — the vet finds them (Plan 03), taps 'Nueva mascota' on their ficha, fills name + species (+ optional photo/details) and saves; and any pet ficha can be corrected via 'Editar' (PAT-01 D-03 path, PAT-02 edit).

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** veterinario independiente, **I want to** registrar, encontrar y mantener al día a mis clientes y sus mascotas reales desde el celular, **so that** pueda llevar mi consulta sin computador, sin recepcionista y sin datos de prueba.

Purpose: Completes multi-pet-per-owner as a first-class flow (PITFALLS "multi-mascota por dueño desde el inicio") and closes PAT-02's edit half, reusing MascotaCamposSection and AppPhotoPicker so both entry points look and behave identically.
Output: MascotaFormScreen in create/edit modes, wired from both fichas; widget tests.
</objective>
